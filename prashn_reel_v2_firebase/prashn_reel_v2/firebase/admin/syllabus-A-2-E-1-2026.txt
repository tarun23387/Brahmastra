1. Computer Fundamentals & Computer Organisation: Classification of computers,
Elements of Computer Processing System, Hardware, Software, CPU, Peripherals,
Storage Media, General organization of a digital computer, Data Representation, fixed
and floating point decimal arithmetic, Bit slice microprocessor, Adders & its types,
